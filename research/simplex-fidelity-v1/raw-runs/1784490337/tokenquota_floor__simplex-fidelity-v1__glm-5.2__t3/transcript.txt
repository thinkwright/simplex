## How it satisfies the rules
- **R1/D1**: `Bucket(capacity, refill_per_second, clock=None)` is importable from `tokenquota.public` and starts full (`_balance = capacity`).
- **R2**: `allow(amount=1)` refills first, consumes and returns `True` when `balance >= amount`, else returns `False` without consuming.
- **R3**: refill adds `elapsed * refill_per_second`, clamps to `capacity`, and treats backward clock movement as zero elapsed time (`elapsed = max(0, now - last)`).
- **R4**: after each *positive-time* refill and capacity clamp, the balance is floored to the nearest integer (`math.floor`) before answering.
- **R5**: `available()` runs the same refill calculation and returns the current balance without consuming.
- **R6/X1**: validates `capacity > 0`, `refill_per_second >= 0`, and `amount > 0` & not boolean; non-numeric inputs and invalid values raise `ValueError` before any state change (no tokens consumed on error).
- **R7**: `allow`/`available` are guarded by a `threading.Lock`, so concurrent successful consumption never exceeds available tokens.
- **C1/E6**: only `math`, `threading`, `time` (standard library) are imported; the supplied `clock` callable is the sole source of time (default `time.monotonic`).
