**Key design points:**
- Constructor validates `capacity > 0` and `refill_per_second >= 0`, rejecting booleans and non-numerics with `ValueError` (R6).
- Bucket starts full (R1); refill uses `elapsed = now - last_time`, treats negative elapsed as 0 (R3), caps at capacity, and preserves fractional tokens exactly (R4).
- `allow(amount)` validates `amount` first (raising `ValueError` without consuming, X1), then refills under the lock and only subtracts on success (R2).
- `available()` performs the same refill and returns the balance without consuming (R5).
- All refill/consume paths are guarded by a single `threading.Lock`, so concurrent successful `allow` calls never exceed available tokens (R7).
- Time source is exclusively the supplied `clock` callable (or `time.monotonic`), satisfying C1.
